Behind The Canvas

Ansh Saxena — Crafting Emotion in Graphite & Charcoal

What began as a relentless devotion to capturing human expression on paper has grown into Ansh Saxena Art Gallery—a trusted fine-art studio with over 150+ commissioned orders delivered across India and internationally to Dubai.

Every portrait is built layer by layer using graded graphite, deep willow & compressed charcoal, and archival Fabriano papers—ensuring zero digital shortcuts and heirloom longevity. Beyond studio commissions, Ansh explores creative storytelling on @anshverse11 and shares speed-sketching tutorials on YouTube.
